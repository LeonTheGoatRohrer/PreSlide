# susceptibility_v02 – Anfragen nach Kartierumrissen (Entwürfe)

**Stand: 18.10.2026.** Entwürfe für den Nutzer, der sie selbst versendet. Platzhalter in eckigen Klammern ausfüllen. Antworten und erhaltene Dateien werden in Phase 1 mit Quelle, Datum und Prüfsumme dokumentiert (`DATA_SOURCES.md`). Bis dahin bleiben beide Inventare `pending_extent`.

Wichtig für die Auswertung: Ein geliefertes Gebiet ist nur dann verwendbar, wenn es **unabhängig von den Rutschungen** festgelegt wurde (abgedeckte Bild- oder Kartierfläche, Gemeindeliste). Ein nachträglich um die Rutschungen gezogener Umriss ist nach der Registrierung nicht zulässig. Deshalb fragen beide Mails ausdrücklich, wie das Gebiet definiert wurde.

## 1. RER2023 (Emilia-Romagna, Mai 2023)

**An:** alessandro.corsini@unimore.it (Korrespondenzautor laut ESSD-Artikel)
**Betreff:** RER2023 landslide inventory – request for the mapped-area boundary

Dear Prof. Corsini,

I am working on a non-commercial research prototype that compares terrain-based landslide susceptibility between rainfall- and earthquake-triggered inventories. Your RER2023 inventory (Pizziolo et al. 2024, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.13742643; ESSD 17, 1055, 2025) is one of the few event inventories mapped systematically over a large area, which makes it very valuable for this comparison.

For the analysis, background (non-landslide) samples must be drawn only inside the area that was actually mapped. Otherwise unmapped ground would be treated as landslide-free. The Zenodo record contains the landslide polygons only. The article states that the area of more than 6,000 km² was segmented by municipal boundaries, but I could not find the boundary itself or the list of municipalities.

Would you be able to share either of the following?

1. the boundary of the mapped area (any GIS format, with its coordinate reference system), or
2. the list of municipalities that were mapped completely (ISTAT codes or names), so that I can rebuild the area from official ISTAT boundaries.

It would also help to know whether parts of these municipalities were excluded from mapping, for example because of cloud cover, missing imagery or urban areas.

I deliberately do not want to derive the area from the landslide polygons themselves (for example as a hull), because that would place the background only where landslides occurred and bias the results.

The data would be used only for this research, with full citation of your dataset and article. I would not redistribute the boundary unless you agree, and I am happy to share the results with you.

Thank you very much for your time and for making the inventory openly available.
